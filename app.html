CsvWhy

Paste a CSV. CsvWhy shows how it parses, where it breaks the RFC 4180 rules, and which line to fix: stray quotes, an unclosed quote that swallows the file, ragged rows, the wrong delimiter.
Follows RFC 4180 (read in full). Rows are read the way Python's csv module reads them, and the result was compared with it on thousands of random inputs; the RFC grammar check was compared with its ABNF turned into a regular expression. Non-ASCII text is accepted (the RFC allows other charsets). Nothing is uploaded; it all runs in your browser.
