CsvWhy
One unclosed quote can turn a whole file into a single cell.
Open the app
A row like 2,Bob,"said "hi" to me" has quotes that were not doubled. The RFC does not allow it, and parsers disagree on what it means. CsvWhy shows the line and the rule: inside quotes, write "" for each quote.

What it does

  • Shows how the CSV parses, row by row, with field counts
  • Lists RFC 4180 problems with line numbers: stray quotes, text after a closing quote, unclosed quotes, line breaks, control characters
  • Flags ragged rows and blank lines, and detects semicolon, tab and pipe files
  • Checked against Python's csv module and the RFC grammar on thousands of random inputs
Follows RFC 4180. Static, client-side, nothing uploaded. Source
